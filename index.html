What I work on

Research

My research asks two central questions: how does RNA–protein molecular grammar govern and modulate the properties of biomolecular condensates, and how does RNA's inherent propensity for self-assembly and condensate formation vary under different external environmental conditions? I use simulations ranging from all-atom models to hydrophobicity-scale (HPS) coarse-grained models, depending on the system of interest.

🧬

All-Atom Simulation of RNA

Atomistic molecular dynamics of structured and homopolymeric RNAs to map energy landscapes, ion-mediated interactions, and reentrant phase behavior in RNA and RNA–polycation systems.

Ongoing
💧

Condensation via All-Atom & HPS Models

Multiscale simulation of biomolecular condensation — from short peptide/RNA fragments at all-atom resolution to hydrophobicity-scale (HPS) coarse-grained models of multiphase protein–RNA assemblies.

Ongoing
